Teaching notes

Increase saturation at constant void ratio to replace air with water. Compare dry and saturated soil. Use Compact soil to reduce air volume while keeping water and solid volumes fixed. Increase solid volume to demonstrate that intensive properties remain unchanged.

Assumptions: water unit weight = 9.81 kN/m³; air weight is negligible; solid particles are incompressible. Compaction is an idealized air-removal process, not a Proctor test model.
